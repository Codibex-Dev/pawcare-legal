Privacy Policy — PawCare

Last updated: 18 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Codibex LLP ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, shares, and protects your information when you use the PawCare mobile application and related services (the "App"). By using the App, you agree to the practices described here.

PawCare is intended for users aged 18 and over. The App does not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.

1. Who we are

The App is operated by Codibex LLP, located at B-8, Third Floor, Sector 2, Noida - 201301, India. For any privacy-related questions, contact admin@codibex.com.

2. Information we collect

Information you provide

  • Account details: your name and email address. You sign in with Google or Apple.
  • Pet and health information you add: a pet's name, photo, species, breed, age, sex, and weight; daily check-ins, symptoms, medications, vaccines, and vet visits; and photos you choose to add.
  • Messages you send in chat, and the replies we send back.

Information collected automatically

  • Location, only when you look for a vet nearby. We use it to run that search. We do not save it on the pet profile.
  • Usage and analytics data: screens viewed, features used, and similar events, used to understand and improve the App.
  • Diagnostic data: crash reports and error logs.
  • Device and technical data: device type, operating system version, app version, and a device or installation identifier.
  • Push notification token: if you enable notifications, a token that lets us deliver them.

3. How we use your information

  • To keep a health record for each dog or cat and offer general guidance based on what you share.
  • To operate your account and the features you choose to use.
  • To send notifications you have enabled.
  • To find open vets near you when you ask.
  • To analyse usage, diagnose problems, and improve the App.
  • To protect against fraud, abuse, and security risks, and to comply with legal obligations.

4. Legal basis for processing

Where Indian law, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, applies, we process your personal data on the basis of the consent you provide when you submit it, and where necessary to provide the services you requested. You may withdraw consent as described under "Your rights". Withdrawing consent may mean we can no longer provide certain features.

6. Data retention

We keep your personal data while your account is active, or as needed to provide the App. When you delete your account, we delete or anonymise your personal data within a reasonable period, except where we must retain records for legal, accounting, or fraud-prevention reasons.

7. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you can:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Request deletion of your data and your account.
  • Withdraw consent you previously gave.
  • Nominate another person to exercise your rights in the event of death or incapacity, as provided under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

Contact admin@codibex.com to exercise these rights.

8. How to delete your data

  • In the App, go to Profile → Account → Delete Account and follow the prompts.
  • Email admin@codibex.com with the subject "Delete my account" from the email address linked to your account.

After deletion, your account data is permanently removed, subject to the limited legal retention above.

9. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures, including encrypted connections and access controls. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

10. Children

The App is not directed to children under 18, and we do not knowingly collect their personal data. If you believe a minor has provided data, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy. When we make material changes, we update the "Last updated" date and, where appropriate, notify you in the App. Continued use after the change means you accept the updated policy.
